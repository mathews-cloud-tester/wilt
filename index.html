How to play
  • Each turn, do one thing: plant a seed, harvest, or pass.
  • Every plant ages each turn. The number on a tile counts down to its bloom.
  • Blooms last only 2 turns — miss the window and the plant wilts into a husk that blocks the cell.
  • Harvest reaps every bloom on the board at once. Each connected group scores (sum of values) × (group size), so time your blooms to fire together, side by side.
  • Harvesting a group of 3+ also clears husks touching that group.
  • Seeds: Quick 🌼 blooms in 2, worth 1 Standard 🌸 blooms in 3, worth 3 Slow 🪻 blooms in 5, worth 7
  • 40 turns. Chase a high score.
